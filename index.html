How a streak counts

Days run on UTC, from midnight to midnight. The contract only compares a workout's day with your last one:

  • The next day adds one to your streak.
  • The same day counts the workout and leaves the streak alone.
  • Any gap starts it again at one.

Tap a day to add a workout, tap again for a second one, a third time to clear it.

Carved, not saved

Runar is named for runes: marks cut into stone so the record outlived the person who made it. This one was carved in Uppland around the year 1050, and it can still be read.

Total workouts, current streak, best streak, points and your last workout day are kept by the contract. Anyone can read them with one call.

No server decides your streak. The contract compares today's day number with your last one, and that is the whole rule.

Join with one transaction. Every matching workout you log in the window counts by itself. After the end day anyone can close it, and the contract names the winner.

Open competition

Anyone can create a challenge and anyone can join. Nothing is staked and the contract never holds funds. Standings are kept as you log, so closing a challenge never replays history.
