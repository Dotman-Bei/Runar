# Runar logo

The mark is **ᚱ, Raidō**, the rune for the letter R, traditionally read as "journey" or "ride". Runes are all straight
strokes because they were cut across wood grain, so the mark has no curves. Its staff and bowl are one folded stroke, and
the leg is a separate straight stroke that reaches past the bowl: a stride, and the day's workout added to the record.

Open `preview.html` to see every version together.

## Colour

| | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#1F2B4D` | Staff, bowl and wordmark on light backgrounds; the app icon's background |
| Tangerine | `#F7941D` | The leg only |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Staff, bowl and wordmark on dark backgrounds |

Use tangerine for the leg and nothing else in the logo. On a tangerine or photographic background, use a one-colour version.

## Spacing and size

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of the staff multiplied by three, on all sides.
- Smallest sizes: logo 20 px tall, mark 16 px tall. Below 24 px on screen, use `favicon.svg` for the mark.
- The gaps between strokes are part of the drawing. Don't close them, outline the mark, add effects or redraw the letters.
